About Miirevyn

Fundamentals First, Always

Founded by former college point guard Jordan Blaze, Miirevyn was built on a simple belief: flashy highlight moves don't win games — real fundamentals do. Every program is built on footwork, mechanics and basketball IQ, not just drills for social media.

  • Certified skills coaches with college & pro playing experience
  • Programs for youth, high school and elite competitive players
  • Video-reviewed shooting mechanics for every player
  • Individualized development plans tracked every season
